PRIVACY POLICY

SlowLock collects nothing.

Last updated 25 August 2026
0
bytes of your data sent anywhere
0

What the app does with your data

SlowLock reads the list of apps installed on your device so it can show them to you when you create a shortcut. That list is read on the device, used to draw the screen, and never leaves the device.

The shortcuts you create — the chosen app, the length of the wait, and the icon style — are stored on the device only, so the app can recreate your shortcuts and delays.

What the app does not do

01 No network requests. The app has no server and sends no data off the device.
02 No analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers or third-party SDKs.
03 No accounts, sign-in, email address or any other personal detail is requested.
04 No record of which apps you open, how often, or how long you use them.
05 Nothing is sold or shared, because there is nothing collected to sell or share.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling SlowLock removes everything it stored. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings has the same effect. There is no copy anywhere else to request or delete.

Children

The app is suitable for any age and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Verifying this yourself

SlowLock is open source. Every claim above can be checked in the source code at github.com/tberchanov/SlowLock.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or sends, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
