HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, once per app.

01
Choose an app
Search your installed apps and pick the one you reach for without thinking.
02
Set the wait
Anywhere from one to thirty seconds. Long enough to notice you opened it.
03
Add the shortcut
It lands on your home screen with the app's own icon, or a gray or inverted one.
WHY IT EXISTS

Blocking apps makes you want them more.

Most habits on a phone are not decisions. The hand moves, the app opens, and a few minutes are gone before anything was chosen. Blockers answer this with a wall, and a wall invites a workaround.

SlowLock does one thing instead: it puts a few empty seconds where the reflex used to be. Nothing is forbidden. If you still want the app after the wait, it opens. Often you will have already put the phone down.

No streaks No counters No shaming

Open source, all of it.

The app makes no network calls, and you can check that yourself. Read the source, build it, or file an issue.
